Our Medical Office Cleaning Services in Houlton

We provide three core service lines for medical and dental facilities in Houlton, WI and the surrounding 25-mile service area. Each is built on clinical-grade disinfection protocols and delivered by crews trained for regulated environments.

Exam Room & Clinical Space Disinfection

Systematic disinfection of examination tables, high-touch surfaces, clinical equipment adjacent areas, and restroom fixtures in patient-accessible spaces. EPA List N products, correct dwell times, documented by room. Suitable for primary care, urgent care, physical therapy, and specialist clinics.

Full service details →

Dental & Specialty Practice Cleaning

Post-procedure cleaning for dental operatories, sterilization room support cleaning, dental unit surface wipe-down protocols, and instrument tray area maintenance. Scheduled around your appointment blocks. Appropriate for general dentistry, oral surgery, orthodontics, and pediatric dental practices.

Full service details →

Waiting Room & Reception Cleaning

High-frequency disinfection of chairs, armrests, door handles, check-in counters, payment terminals, pen/stylus stations, children's areas, and magazine racks. Managed to reduce pathogen transfer in shared patient spaces where infection control is often overlooked.

Full service details →

How Services Are Delivered

All three service lines can be scheduled independently or combined into a single cleaning program. Most practices combine exam room disinfection with waiting room cleaning on the same visit schedule. Dental practices often add operatory-specific protocols as a distinct service. We build the scope around your layout and patient volume, not around a pre-packaged bundle.
