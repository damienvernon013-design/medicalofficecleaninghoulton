Dental & Specialty Practice Cleaning in Houlton, WI

Dental practices operate under infection-control standards that exceed general medical office requirements. The CDC Guidelines for Infection Control in Dental Health-Care Settings and OSHA bloodborne pathogen regulations set a floor that standard commercial cleaning cannot meet. Our dental cleaning program is built to that floor, not below it.

Operatory Cleaning

Between-appointment and end-of-day operatory cleaning covers dental chair surfaces, delivery unit, overhead light handle, bracket table, cuspidor (where applicable), and countertops. All surfaces receive disinfectant at appropriate concentration and held to labeled dwell time before wiping. Chair articulation controls and headrest are included — these are high-frequency touch points that are often missed in general cleaning.

Sterilization Room Support

We clean the exterior surfaces of sterilization room counters and non-instrument areas. We do not handle instrument sterilization or autoclave operation — that remains your clinical staff's scope. We maintain the cleanliness of the surrounding environment: counter surfaces, sinks, cabinetry exteriors, and floor.

Dental Unit Surfaces

Air-water syringe handles, high-volume evacuation handles, saliva ejector holders, and X-ray equipment housings are included in the surface wipe-down protocol. These surfaces are touched by gloved clinical hands during every procedure and represent a cross-contamination risk if cleaning is inconsistent.

Scheduling Around Appointments

Dental cleaning is almost always scheduled after last patient of the day, or on days when the schedule closes early. For practices with morning hygiene blocks and afternoon restorative blocks, we can accommodate a between-block clean for operatories if your schedule and layout allow. This is discussed and documented in the written scope — it is not assumed.

Specialty Practices

We also clean oral surgery suites, orthodontic practices, and pediatric dental facilities in this region. Each has slightly different surface profiles and, in the case of oral surgery, additional considerations around post-procedure cleanup. Specialty requirements are documented during the walkthrough.
