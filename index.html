Medical Office Cleaning for the St. Croix Corridor

Houlton sits at the Wisconsin end of the I-94 bridge crossing into the Twin Cities metro. That geography means practices here often serve patients commuting from Hudson, North Hudson, and the surrounding townships — and it means your facility is visible to a patient base that can compare you to urban alternatives. Clean, professionally maintained exam rooms and waiting areas are part of that comparison.

We provide clinical-grade cleaning to medical offices, dental practices, specialty clinics, and urgent care facilities. Our crews work under written disinfection protocols, use EPA List N-approved products appropriate to each surface, and understand why cross-contamination control matters differently in a clinical setting than in a standard office environment.

Why Medical Facilities Choose Us

Practices that answer to a licensing board need a cleaning contractor who understands that difference. Standard janitorial contracts written for offices do not specify dwell times, do not address cross-contamination routes between exam rooms, and do not train crews on HIPAA-relevant behavior in clinical spaces. We do.
