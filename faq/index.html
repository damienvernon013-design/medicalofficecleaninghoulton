Medical Office Cleaning FAQs for Houlton Businesses

What is the difference between medical office cleaning and standard commercial cleaning?

Standard commercial cleaning uses general-purpose cleaners appropriate for offices, retail spaces, and similar environments. Medical office cleaning uses EPA List N-registered disinfectants — products tested and approved for use against pathogens including SARS-CoV-2 — applied at correct concentrations with proper dwell times before wiping. It also requires crew training on cross-contamination control between patient contact areas and HIPAA-relevant behavior in clinical spaces.

Are you insured and bonded?

Yes. We carry general liability insurance appropriate for commercial and clinical cleaning operations. We are bonded, and all crew members working in your facility are covered under workers' compensation. A certificate of insurance is provided with every written quote. See our insurance and bonding page for details.

Do you use EPA List N disinfectants?

Yes. EPA List N is the EPA's register of disinfectants that meet the criteria for use against SARS-CoV-2. We use List N-registered products appropriate to each surface type in your facility. Dwell times are followed as specified on the product label. We can provide product data sheets on request.

How do you handle HIPAA compliance in our facility?

Our crews receive HIPAA awareness training covering patient privacy expectations in clinical spaces: not accessing or reading visible patient information, maintaining discretion in treatment areas if patients are encountered, and understanding which areas require access restrictions. We do not certify HIPAA compliance — that is the responsibility of your practice — but we ensure cleaning staff behavior does not create additional exposure.

How do we get started?

Call us at (866) 958-8773 Monday through Friday, 8:30am to 5:00pm, or submit the online quote form. We schedule a walkthrough, produce a written scope of work within two business days, and can typically begin service within two weeks of a signed agreement.

Do you clean after hours or on weekends?

Most of our medical office clients schedule cleaning after patient hours — typically late afternoon or evening on weekdays. Weekend availability is limited and generally not offered as a standard schedule. If your practice has specific timing requirements, discuss them during the walkthrough.
