Dental Practice Cleaning: What Makes It Different from General Medical Cleaning

Operatories, sterilization rooms, and appointment-driven scheduling all shape a dental-specific protocol.

Operatory-Specific Protocols

Dental operatories have a distinct set of high-touch surfaces — dental unit surfaces, light handles, chair controls, and instrument tray areas — that a general exam room disinfection protocol doesn't cover in the same detail. Cleaning between patients and at close of day needs to account for this layout specifically.

Sterilization-Room-Adjacent Cleaning

We don't handle instrument sterilization itself — that's your clinical team's regulated process — but the surfaces around the sterilization area need consistent disinfection to avoid becoming a cross-contamination point.

Appointment-Driven Scheduling

Unlike a standard office cleaned after hours on a fixed schedule, dental practices often need cleaning scheduled around appointment blocks — a quick between-patient wipe-down distinct from the full end-of-day clean. Discuss your appointment flow with your provider so the schedule matches your actual patient turnover.

Practice Types We Support
