How to Get an Accurate Medical Office Cleaning Quote

A few minutes of preparation before your first call produces a far more useful quote.

Know Your Square Footage and Room Count

Medical cleaning is priced differently than general office cleaning, and exam room or operatory count matters as much as total square footage. Walk your space and count exam rooms, operatories, restrooms, and any specialty areas like sterilization rooms before you request quotes.

Define Your Scope Before the Walkthrough

Write down what you need before inviting a provider in: which rooms, which tasks, how often, and any compliance requirements from your licensing body or insurer. This prevents a provider from quoting a stripped-down service and prevents you from accepting something that won't meet your actual infection-control obligations.

Get Multiple Quotes — and Compare Scope, Not Just Price

A single quote gives you a number. Several quotes give you a market, and more importantly, let you compare what's actually included. The cheapest quote in a medical setting is often the one missing EPA List N disinfectant use, documented dwell times, or bloodborne pathogen training for staff — details that matter far more here than in a standard office clean.

Ask What's NOT Included

Always ask a provider to tell you what falls outside their standard quote — regulated waste handling, sterilization equipment cleaning, and after-hours emergency response are common gaps. Surfacing this before you sign avoids a bad surprise later.

Ask About Their Medical-Specific Experience

A cleaning company that does excellent work in retail or office spaces may not have the training or protocols for a clinical environment. Ask specifically about experience with medical or dental facilities, bloodborne pathogen training, and EPA List N product use.
