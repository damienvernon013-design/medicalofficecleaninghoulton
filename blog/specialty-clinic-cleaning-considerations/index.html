Cleaning Considerations for Specialty Clinics

Equipment-heavy exam spaces need a protocol that accounts for shared surfaces beyond a standard exam table.

Shared Equipment Surfaces

Physical therapy, chiropractic, and similar specialty clinics often have shared treatment tables, exercise equipment, and therapy tools that see multiple patients per day. These surfaces need disinfection protocols that go beyond a standard exam table wipe-down.

Longer Appointment Times, Different Turnover

Specialty appointments often run longer than a standard primary care visit, meaning fewer patients per room per day but more surface contact time per visit. The cleaning cadence should reflect actual turnover, not assume the same frequency as a high-volume urgent care.

Facility Layout Variation

A specialty clinic layout — open treatment areas, equipment rooms, private consultation rooms — varies more than a standard exam-room-based practice. A provider should walk your specific layout rather than quoting from a generic medical office template.

What to Discuss With Your Provider

Bring your specific equipment and layout to the conversation. A provider unfamiliar with shared treatment equipment may miss surfaces that matter most for your patient population.
