Insured, Bonded and 22 Years Serving Houlton

Medical and dental practices that hire a cleaning service are extending access to their facility to people who will be alone in exam rooms, near patient records, and in contact with surfaces that affect patient health outcomes. The credentials below are what responsible credential-checking looks like before signing a cleaning agreement.

General Liability Insurance

We carry general liability insurance appropriate for commercial and clinical cleaning operations. This coverage responds to property damage or bodily injury claims arising from our work in your facility. A certificate of insurance naming your practice as an additional insured is available upon request and is provided with every written quote.

Bonding

We maintain a commercial surety bond. Bonding provides a financial backstop in the event of employee dishonesty — theft or misappropriation by a crew member while on your premises. Bonded status is a reasonable baseline requirement for any cleaning contractor with unsupervised facility access, and we meet it.

Workers' Compensation

All crew members working in client facilities are covered under workers' compensation. This protects you from liability if a crew member is injured while cleaning your space. Do not accept a cleaning contract from a contractor who cannot confirm current workers' comp coverage — the liability exposure for an uninsured contractor injury on your premises falls on you.

22 Years in Business

Continuous insurance and bonding over 22 years in business means no gaps, no lapses, and no periods operating uncovered. A cleaning contractor who went uninsured for two years and re-entered the market insured last month presents a different risk profile than one who has maintained continuous coverage through two decades of operations.
