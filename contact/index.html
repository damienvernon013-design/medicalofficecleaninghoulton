Contact Houlton Medical Office Cleaning

The quickest way to reach us is by phone during business hours, or by submitting the quote form — which we respond to by next business day.

Phone

(866) 958-8773
Available Monday through Friday, 8:30am to 5:00pm Central. If you reach voicemail, leave your name, facility name, and the best time to call back. We return calls the same business day when possible, or the following morning.

Email

ops@thequotemasters.com
Email is monitored during business hours. Quote requests, scope-of-work questions, and service inquiries are all appropriate for email. We do not use the email address for scheduling or urgent matters — call for those.

Hours

Monday through Friday, 8:30am–5:00pm. We do not staff phones on weekends or holidays. The online quote form is available at any hour, and we respond to all submissions by next business day.

Service Area

We serve Houlton, WI and communities within 25 miles, including Hudson, North Hudson, Somerset, and Saint Joseph. We do not publish a street address — we operate as a mobile service area business, and crews dispatch from multiple locations.

Preparing for your call: Having the following ready will make your first conversation faster — approximate square footage of your facility, number of exam rooms or operatories, your current cleaning schedule (or what you are looking for), and whether you have specific disinfection requirements from a licensing body or insurer.
