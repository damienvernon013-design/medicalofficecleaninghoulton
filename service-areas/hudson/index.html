About Hudson and the Practices We Serve Here

  • Hudson is the St. Croix County seat, home to county government offices, the county courthouse, and a concentration of professional services along Third Street.
  • The Hudson Medical Center on Stageline Road anchors a healthcare corridor that includes several specialty practices and ancillary health services — meaning patients in the area are accustomed to medical appointments and facilities that meet a higher standard.
  • Hudson's position at the I-94/WI-35 interchange makes it the commercial hub for St. Croix County, with drive times to downtown St. Paul under 30 minutes. Practices here compete with Twin Cities facilities for patient retention.
