How Service Area Coverage Works

Practices in communities surrounding Houlton receive the same written scope of work, the same disinfection protocols, and the same crew consistency as Houlton clients. We do not operate a tiered service model where remote locations receive a simplified protocol. The only difference is drive time, which is factored into scheduling — we do not charge a per-mile fee.

For practices located outside the listed communities, call (866) 958-8773 to confirm coverage before requesting a quote. We can often accommodate facilities slightly beyond the standard 25-mile radius depending on schedule density in that area.
