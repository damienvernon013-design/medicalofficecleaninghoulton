Dental & Specialty Practice Cleaning in North Hudson, WI

Dental practices in North Hudson, WI operate under the same CDC infection-control guidelines and OSHA bloodborne pathogen regulations as practices in any other location. We bring the same clinical cleaning protocols to North Hudson that we use in Houlton — approximately ~8 miles from our base.

A North Hudson Dental Practice Example

A solo-practitioner dental office near the North Hudson commercial strip — one operatory, approximately 900 sq ft — running three days per week with post-close cleaning and dental unit surface protocol.

For this type of facility, the cleaning scope covers post-close operatory disinfection (chair surfaces, delivery unit, overhead light handle, bracket table, countertops), sterilization room counter and non-instrument surfaces, and dental unit handle wipe-down. Floor care and restroom disinfection are included per visit.

What Dental Practice Cleaning Includes

  • Operatory surfaces: chair, delivery unit, overhead light handle, bracket table
  • Dental unit surfaces: air-water syringe handles, HVE handles, saliva ejector holders
  • Sterilization room counters and exterior surfaces (not instrument handling)
  • X-ray equipment housings and counter areas
  • Restrooms serving patient and staff areas
  • Reception and waiting area surfaces

Scheduling in North Hudson
