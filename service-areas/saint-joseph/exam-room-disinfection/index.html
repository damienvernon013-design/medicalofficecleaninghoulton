Exam Room & Clinical Space Disinfection in Saint Joseph, WI

We provide exam room and clinical space disinfection to medical practices in Saint Joseph, WI, approximately ~18 miles from Houlton. The same written protocols, EPA List N disinfectants, and crew consistency we use in Houlton apply here.

A Saint Joseph Example

A 1,200 sq ft rural health clinic serving Saint Joseph Township — two exam rooms and a shared treatment area — functioning as the primary care access point for patients who have no closer alternative, requiring consistent three-day-per-week cleaning with documented protocols for state licensing review.

The protocol for this type of facility: end-of-day disinfection of all exam surfaces (table, patient contact areas, high-touch handles and fixtures), hard floor mopping with clinical-grade solution, restroom disinfection, and a brief written check completed by the crew confirming all rooms were serviced. Discrepancies — a supply shortage, a damaged surface that needs attention — are reported by the next morning.

What the Service Includes

  • Examination table disinfection and paper roll replacement
  • High-touch surface disinfection (door handles, light switches, equipment housings, wall-mounted dispensers)
  • Counter and sink areas
  • Biohazard-adjacent surface treatment
  • Hard-surface floor cleaning with disinfectant mop
  • Clinical corridor restrooms

Scheduling in Saint Joseph

Drive time from Houlton to Saint Joseph is ~18 miles. We schedule Saint Joseph facilities into our St. Croix County route — typically serviced Monday through Friday evenings, or on a three-day schedule for lower-volume practices. Exact schedule is documented in the written scope.
